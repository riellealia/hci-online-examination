How grades are calculated

Your percentage is transmuted from the raw marks: (marks ÷ total × 50) + 50. A grade of 75% is the passing mark and converts to a rating of 3.00.

97–100 = 1.0094–96 = 1.25 91–93 = 1.5088–90 = 1.75 85–87 = 2.0082–84 = 2.25 79–81 = 2.5076–78 = 2.75 75 = 3.00 (passing) 70–74 = 4.00 (failed) below 70 = 5.00 (failed)
